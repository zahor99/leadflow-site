European keyword research · CLH Google Ads · 31 Aug 2026

Same buyer, a third of the click price.

In-depth keyword sweep across 10 Northern/Western European markets plus native-language keyword sets for German, Dutch, Danish, Swedish and French. The finding: the exact commercial queries that cost $34–88 per click in the UK cost $7–14 across the continent — and Germany alone has a larger addressable search market than the UK.

Combined Northern/Western Europe money volume ≈ 15,000+ searches/mo at $6–14 CPC, versus the UK's ~4,800 at $18–35. Roughly 3× the addressable clicks at a third of the price.

What this does to the budget model: the base-case funnel (4% click→lead × 60% lead→meeting) at a ~$11 European blended CPC gives a cost per booked meeting of ≈ $460 — versus $1,201 for the UK blend. CAC becomes ≈ $2,290 at 20% close, ≈ $1,530 at 30%, ≈ $920 at 50%. The same $4,500–6,500/month recommended budget buys ~12–14 meetings instead of ~5.

Germany is the single biggest opportunity in the sweep: real volume in both languages, mid-single-digit to low-teens CPCs, and the privacy/GDPR angle is culturally native — Datenschutz sells in Germany like nowhere else.

Plus the English-language German market: "ai consulting/consultant" 1,300/mo @ $13.52, "ai for business" 480 @ $14, "ai roadmap" 170 @ $4.54, "ai audit" 140 @ $6.78. A German landing page also unlocks Austria (ai consulting 90 @ $9.65 EN + German searches) and Switzerland (ki beratung 170 @ $21.92, wealthy market) at near-zero extra cost — one DACH campaign group.

The "eu ai act" 12,100/mo is a different animal: $3.23 clicks at enormous volume, but the intent is largely informational — companies reading up on the law, not hiring yet. Don't point it at the meeting CTA. Point it at a German version of the CEO's AI Leverage Blueprint white paper and nurture. It's the cheapest qualified-audience builder in the entire dataset (also 2,400/mo NL, 1,000 SE, 720 DK).

3 · The other translated markets — worth it or not

Netherlands — English page, Dutch keywords

The Dutch search "ai consultancy / ai consultant" (880/mo @ $14.11) in English loanwords; Dutch B2B comfortably converts on English pages. Run NL as an English campaign with both English and Dutch-loanword keywords (ai bureau 140, ai implementatie 170, ai voor bedrijven 110). Translation optional later. "ai wetgeving" (210 @ $3.95) and "ai privacy" (110) feed the compliance angle.

Denmark — small, cheap, but handle with care

Volumes are modest (ai konsulent 140+140 @ $7.74, ai rådgivning 90, gdpr og ai ~40 EN) and clicks are cheap. ⚠ Any localisation that leans on the flagship engagement's home market risks making that client identifiable — which contract confidentiality forbids. A Danish page is therefore gated on the client's written sign-off of the exact wording; until then, serve Denmark through the English EU-North campaign only.

Sweden · Norway · Finland — English, opportunistic

ai konsult(er) 520/mo @ $7.87 SV, artificiell intelligens företag 210 @ $5.26; EN terms $7–12. Add as English campaigns with local keyword sprinkle; translate nothing yet.

France — cheapest clicks, biggest caveat

agence ia 1,900 @ $5.65 · consultant ia 590 @ $4.86 · audit ia 320 @ $9.88 · rgpd ia + ia et rgpd 280 @ $7–10. Genuinely cheap and the RGPD angle mirrors the revamp. But French buyers expect French sales conversations end-to-end. Only enter if Mo wants to sell in French — otherwise park it.

Ireland — free extension

Same language, same page; ai consulting 90 @ $22.65. Add to the UK campaign's geo-targeting, done.

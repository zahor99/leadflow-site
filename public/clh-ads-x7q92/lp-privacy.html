Straight answers

Asked before every engagement.

Where does our data go?

It stays in your cloud tenant, on your accounts and agreements. Where a model call could involve personal data, an anonymisation gate strips it first. What is logged, what may leave, and who can approve what are design decisions we make with you — in writing — before go-live.

Do you replace our IT team or partners?

No. We design around the systems, permissions and technology partners you already have, with tightly scoped access.

Why are your case studies anonymised?

Because our contracts promise clients confidentiality and we keep that promise — it's the same discipline we'd apply to your data. Named references are available on request where client agreements permit.

What does the Operating Map cost?

Nothing. It's how we scope — a structured view of where AI can help your company and where it must not go. If a first project makes sense, you'll see it there; if it doesn't, the map is still yours.

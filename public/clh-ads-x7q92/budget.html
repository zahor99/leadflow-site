What a customer costs, and what to budget.

Funnel economics for the recommended UK campaigns, from click to signed client — with customer acquisition cost at 20%, 30% and 50% meeting-to-close rates. CPCs are live Google Ads data; conversion rates are stated assumptions to be replaced by real numbers within the first two months.

Your three close-rate cases

Volume ceilings from UK search volumes at a realistic ~20–25% impression share and ~6–8% CTR: Campaign A (Diagnostic + Compliance, ~1,220 searches/mo) ≈ 80 clicks/mo; Campaign B (Core consulting, ~3,000) ≈ 140 clicks/mo; Campaign C (Firm seekers, ~560) ≈ 40 clicks/mo. You cannot usefully spend more than ~$8K/mo in the UK on these clusters — beyond that, the move is the US (5–10× volume, mostly lower competition).

Payback check: a CLH retainer runs €10–20K/month gross; after the delivery wage, roughly €5–10K/month stays as contribution. Even the worst planning-case CAC ($6,000 ≈ €5,500 at 20% close on the base scenario) pays back in under 6 weeks. A 12-month client is worth €60–120K in contribution — an LTV:CAC of roughly 10–20×. The economics are not the risk; the landing-page conversion rate is. That's why the page-as-is row exists: launching before the revamp roughly 2.4× every cost in this document.

4 · Budget tiers

Pilot · prove the funnel
$1,500 /mo · months 1–2
  • Campaign A only ($25–35/day)
  • ~80 clicks → ~2 meetings/mo (base)
  • First customer in ~2–3 months at 30% close
  • Total risk to a read on the funnel: ~$3,000
Recommended · months 2–4
$4,500–6,500 /mo
  • Campaigns A + B (B gated on the revamped page)
  • ~220 clicks → ~5 meetings/mo (base)
  • ~1–1.5 customers/mo at 30% close, CAC ≈ $4,000
  • Enough click volume (~450 over 2 months) to measure the true conversion rate and re-run this model with real numbers
Scale · once CAC is proven
$8,000–12,000 /mo
  • Add Campaign C + the US versions of clusters A/B (5–10× volume, mostly Low competition)
  • ~8–12 meetings/mo
  • Capacity check first: at 30% close this signs 2–4 clients/mo — delivery must be able to absorb it
Two honest caveats. (1) The conversion rates are assumptions — defensible for B2B services search traffic, but assumptions. The pilot's job is to replace them: ~250 clicks gives a usable read on click→lead. Judge nothing before ~150 clicks per campaign. (2) Meetings arrive 1–3 weeks behind spend and clients 2–8 weeks behind meetings — commit to a 3-month horizon and ~$15–20K total working budget for the recommended path, or don't start; a $1,500 one-month test at these CPCs reads as noise.

5 · The plan in one line each

  • Month 1: ship the revamped page → launch Campaign A at $1,500/mo → instrument form + booking as conversions.
  • Month 2: add Campaign B at $3,000–5,000/mo once the page converts ≥3% → keep the $88-CPC "services/firms" ad group bid-capped.
  • Month 3: re-run this model with measured rates → decide: scale UK, open US, or fix the funnel stage that underperforms.
  • Kill criteria: click→lead under 1.5% after 250 clicks on the revamped page, or two months with zero meetings — stop and fix the page, not the bids.

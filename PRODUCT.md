# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Engineering and operations leaders at mid-size to enterprise companies (US & EU) evaluating an outside partner to build AI systems — RAG, agent platforms, ERP/CRM integration. They have usually seen AI demos stall before production and are skeptical of agency hype. Secondary: founders of funded SMBs who want a senior engineer, not a bench of juniors.

## Product Purpose

leadflowautomation.net is the front door for a founder-led AI engineering practice. Its single conversion goal is a booked 30-minute Calendly call with the engineer himself. Every section exists to move a skeptical technical buyer one scroll closer to that call.

## Brand Personality

Senior engineer speaking plainly. Calm technical confidence, proof over promises, zero sales-speak. The voice of someone who has shipped into production at a multi-billion-dollar manufacturer and doesn't need to shout about it.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI agency" template sites: gradient-drenched heroes, buzzword salad, stock robot imagery.
- SaaS stat-tile grids (big number + tiny label + gradient accent).
- Faceless-agency voice ("our team of experts"); this practice is one named engineer plus collaborators.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Every credential and number on the page is true and verifiable; nothing decorative pretending to be evidence.
2. **The engineer is the brand.** Founder-led voice and imagery; the person you scope with is the person who builds.
3. **Production, not demo.** Show shipped systems and their constraints (approval gates, citations, refusal on low confidence) rather than aspirational mockups.
4. **Quiet dark surface, color as signal.** Near-black backgrounds, Inter, the wave-mark blue/pink used sparingly for emphasis and interaction — never as wallpaper.
5. **Each fold earns the next.** One idea per section, paced toward the Calendly CTA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected globally (already enforced in global.css); semantic landmarks and alt text on all meaningful imagery.

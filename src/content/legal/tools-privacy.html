Privacy notice

Who is responsible: BioInsight Lab Inc.

What we keep

  • Your e-mail address and company name, to run your account, send sign-in codes, weekly digest e-mails and work notices, and record that you accepted the terms.
  • If you sign up on our website: your name, e-mail address, company name and website, the consent ticks you gave and when, and the state of your sign-up.
  • Your name and your own e-mail address, if you add them, and a one-time confirmation code for a new address (stored scrambled, valid 30 minutes).
  • What you add and what the checks return: website addresses, keywords, competitor domains, the business name and location for map checks, search results, audit findings, backlink counts and the digests built from them.
  • What we read with your permission: the public pages of your website (our reader names itself LeadFlowBot and follows your robots.txt), kept as a short text digest per workspace, and, only for profiles you confirm as yours and only with your go, your public social profiles.
  • Brands and audience profiles: brand name, website, colours, fonts, logo link, voice notes, claims with their sources, products, social links, and audience pains, wants, objections, where they are and the evidence quotes with their sources.
  • Jobs: the title, the inputs given to start it, its stages, results and links.
  • Review pages: the items and texts on them, any images, the decisions, comments and edited texts, the name a reviewer types (optional), and when the page was first opened.
  • Content-rule settings and a log of who changed them and when.
  • A log of the e-mails we sent (address, type, time), kept 2 days to limit how many we send.
  • Usage records (credits bought and used) and basic server logs for security.

Where and with whom

  • Stored on our server: United States.
  • The search data provider receives the domains, page addresses, keywords and map coordinates of each check, and no contact details. Brands, audience profiles, jobs and review pages are not sent to it.
  • When we read public social profiles you confirmed, our data collection provider receives only those profile addresses.
  • Sign-in codes, digest e-mails, work notices and confirmation codes are sent through our business e-mail provider. A notice contains the page or job title, its link and the job ID.
  • Inside your workspace, everyone who uses it can see its review pages and job results; anyone you share a link with can open that page until it expires.
  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. No advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling of data.
  • The names of our providers are given on request at gergely@startleadflowautomation.com.

Digest and review pages

Digest and review pages set no cookies and load nothing from other sites. Digest pages stop working after 90 days and are then deleted, and you can switch a digest link off at any time. Review pages stop working at the end of the date printed on them (90 days) and are then deleted with their images and decisions.

How long

As long as you use the service, with these limits: review pages 90 days; finished jobs 365 days after they finish; the e-mail log 2 days. Ask us and within 7 days we delete your sites, check results, digest pages, jobs, review pages, brands and audience profiles, what we read from your website and profiles, contact details and rule settings; we keep your account name and e-mail and the record of credits for our accounts as the law requires.

Your rights

Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data at gergely@startleadflowautomation.com. We answer within 7 days. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
